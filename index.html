Supported letters
J and Z involve motion traces rather than a static handshape, so they're excluded. Green dot = you've trained that letter. G/Q and K/P share a handshape and are told apart by whether your hand points sideways/up or down — keep your wrist visible so that reads correctly.
Runs entirely in your browser. Hand landmarks come from Google's MediaPipe Hands model (loaded from jsDelivr); letter classification is a rule-based matcher over palm-relative geometry (finger curl angles, thumb position in a palm-local coordinate frame, fingertip distances), with an optional per-user k-nearest-neighbor layer built from poses you capture in the Train panel. No video or captured pose data leaves your device. Treat this as a demo / learning tool, not a certified interpreter.
